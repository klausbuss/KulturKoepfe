The public perception of the museums is determined by exhibitions and special events.

The permanent exhibition shows a representative cross-section of its own collection. Selected objects are presented in special temporary exhibitions in constantly changing theme contexts.

More: Special Exhibitions ArchiveMehr: Archiv der Sonderausstellungen

Education and communication are primary components of our work in the museum. In addition to more traditional means of communicating, such as object labels, catalogues, public lectures and guided tours, there are today many, e.g. digital and interactive, communication formats as well as educational opportunities available for various target audiences, in particular for children.
